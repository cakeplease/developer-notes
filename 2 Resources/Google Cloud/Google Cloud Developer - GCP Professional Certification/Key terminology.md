**Agility** - ability to move fast and respond quickly to business needs, quickly deliver software, quickly provision infrastructure
**Global reach and geo-distribution** - the ability to expand to new markets and deploy applications to data centers around the world quickly
**Availability** - are applications available when users need them
**Latency** - time taken for a request to travel between user and server, reduced by serving users from nearby locations

### Cost
**Capital expenditure (CapEx)** - large upfront investment
**Operational Expenditure (OpEx)** - ongoing cost for renting resources (or services) as needed
**Pay as you go** - trade capital expense (CapEx) for variable expense (OpEx). Pay only for the resources you actually use.
**Economies of scale** - buying resources in large quantities lowers the cost per unit
**Avoid undifferentiated heavy lifting** - spend more time on what makes your business unique
